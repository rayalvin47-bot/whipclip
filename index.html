4Background music (optional)

Upload your own royalty-free track. It'll automatically duck (get quieter) under your speech and come back up in the gaps — no need to time it yourself.

Want a specific sound instead? Download one free from mixkit.co or pixabay.com and upload it here with the exact second it should play.
