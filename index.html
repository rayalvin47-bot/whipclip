How to use Whipclip

A quick walkthrough for your first clip.
1Upload your video

Tap "Choose file" and pick a video already saved on your device.

2Pick your clip

Play the video, pause where you want your clip to start, tap "Use playhead." Do the same for the end point.

3Add subtitles

Tap "Auto-generate captions" to transcribe your speech automatically, or add lines by hand.

4Optional extras

Add background music (auto-balanced under your voice), sound effects, or a freeze-frame text moment — all optional.

5Export

Tap Export. The first time, it downloads a processing engine (~30MB) — after that it's cached and faster.

6Download

Once done, preview your clip and tap "Download your clip" to save it to your device.
